# Development Roadmap — Version 0.2 to 0.8

Version 0.1 established the foundation of the game.

The objective of the following milestones is **not** to add as many features as possible. Each version should deepen the core gameplay loop while maintaining the project's guiding principle:

> **Flying, fighting, salvaging and building should all become progressively more satisfying.**

---

# Version 0.2 — Salvage & Materials

## Theme

Destroyed ships become opportunities.

This version replaces the placeholder "scrap" economy with a proper material system and makes salvaging a satisfying part of combat.

## Goals

### Materials

Replace generic Scrap with real resources.

Initial materials:

* Steel Alloy
* Electronics
* Reactor Components

Future materials can include:

* Alien Biomatter
* Rare Crystals
* Exotic Alloys
* Ancient Components

---

### Builder Costs

Ship modules should require specific materials rather than generic currency.

Example:

Engine Mk1

* 20 Steel Alloy
* 8 Electronics

Laser Cannon

* 15 Steel Alloy
* 12 Electronics
* 3 Reactor Components

---

### Salvage

Destroyed ships should drop physical salvage.

Improve visual readability.

Players should instantly recognise valuable drops through:

* colour
* glow
* silhouette

rather than reading text.

---

### Extractor Beam

Continue improving the extractor beam.

It should feel satisfying to use.

Potential improvements:

* stronger beam effects
* collection sounds
* objects physically moving toward the ship
* slight pull resistance for heavier objects

---

### Pirate AI Improvements

Pirates should attack when:

* the player enters detection range
* the player fires upon them

Introduce simple AI states:

* Idle
* Suspicious
* Alert
* Retreating (future)

---

## Success Criteria

Players actively seek out wrecks because salvaging is enjoyable.

---

# Version 0.3 — Ship Systems & Energy

## Theme

Ships become engineered machines rather than collections of modules.

## Goals

Introduce an energy system.

Every active module consumes power.

Examples:

* Engines
* Lasers
* Shields
* Tractor Beam
* Missiles

Power is generated by reactors.

Ships without enough power must make choices.

---

### New Modules

* Reactor Mk1
* Battery Mk1

Future:

* Capacitors
* Cooling Systems

---

### Gameplay

Players should begin making engineering decisions.

Examples:

* Bigger reactor?
* More batteries?
* Fewer weapons?
* Faster engines?

---

## Success Criteria

Ship design starts becoming strategic rather than simply adding more weapons.

---

# Version 0.4 — Combat Evolution

## Theme

Every fight feels different.

## Goals

Improve enemy variety.

Initial pirate archetypes:

### Raider

Fast

Light armour

Short range

---

### Gunship

Slow

Heavy armour

Powerful cannons

---

### Missile Boat

Maintains distance

Uses lock-on missiles

---

### Scout

Detects player quickly

Calls nearby allies (future)

---

### Missile Lock System

Implement target locking.

Missiles should feel distinct from lasers.

Lasers reward aim.

Missiles reward positioning.

---

### Combat Feedback

Improve:

* explosions
* sparks
* engine failures
* recoil
* impact effects

---

## Success Criteria

Players change tactics depending on the enemy they're fighting.

---

# Version 0.5 — Technology & Factions

## Theme

Progress through discovery.

## Goals

Introduce faction identity.

Initial factions:

* Pirates
* Corporate Alliance
* Ancient Civilisation

Each faction has:

* unique ship design
* unique technology
* unique salvage
* unique visual style

---

### Reverse Engineering

Technology is discovered.

Not purchased.

Recover unknown components.

Research them.

Unlock manufacturing.

---

### Manufacturers

Introduce manufacturer identities.

Examples:

Atlas Heavy Industries

* powerful
* heavy
* high recoil

Nova Precision

* accurate
* efficient
* lightweight

Black Market Foundry

* unstable
* experimental
* dangerous

---

## Success Criteria

Players become excited by discovering new technology rather than simply collecting resources.

---

# Version 0.6 — The Living Universe

## Theme

Travelling is rewarding.

## Goals

Introduce the first space station.

Functions:

* trade
* repair
* research
* build

---

### Warp Gates

Add handcrafted warp gates between star systems.

Warp travel should feel cinematic.

---

### New Locations

Examples:

* asteroid fields
* derelict stations
* abandoned wrecks
* nebulae

---

### Trading

Allow materials to be bought and sold.

Prices may eventually vary by location.

---

## Success Criteria

Players travel because they want to explore, not because a mission tells them to.

---

# Version 0.7 — Mystery & Discovery

## Theme

The universe becomes intriguing.

## Goals

Introduce unexplained discoveries.

Examples:

* strange signals
* ancient ruins
* unknown probes
* derelict megastructures
* impossible anomalies

Not every discovery should have an immediate explanation.

Curiosity should drive exploration.

---

### Research

Scanning and investigation reveal more over time.

The player gradually understands the universe.

---

## Success Criteria

Players leave their planned route simply because something interesting appeared.

---

# Version 0.8 — Atmosphere & Polish

## Theme

Transform a fun prototype into a believable universe.

## Goals

Improve immersion.

Focus on:

* lighting
* engine trails
* weapon effects
* explosions
* nebula rendering
* UI polish
* audio
* ambient music
* camera feel
* planet approach effects

---

### Ship Identity

Ships should visibly tell their story.

Examples:

* battle damage
* repaired hull plates
* faction technology
* salvaged components
* unique silhouettes

Players should become emotionally attached to their ship.

---

## Success Criteria

A screenshot of the game should immediately look distinctive and recognisable.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every feature added must answer at least one of these questions:

* Does this make flying more enjoyable?
* Does this make combat more memorable?
* Does this make exploration more rewarding?
* Does this make salvaging more satisfying?
* Does this make ship building more meaningful?
* Does this make the player's ship feel more personal?

If the answer is **no**, the feature should be reconsidered or postponed.

---

# Future Beyond Version 0.8

Only after these milestones are complete should development move towards:

* richer storylines
* advanced faction interactions
* dynamic economy
* drop-in/drop-out co-op
* shared-ship crew gameplay inspired by FTL
* AI crew members
* end-game content

The objective is to create an exceptional single-player experience first.

Multiplayer should enhance an already excellent game, never compensate for an incomplete one.

WHY GREYSCALE
Every shape is drawn in white/grey with no hue baked in. Sprite2D.modulate multiplies each pixel by a colour, so white becomes exactly that colour and the dark capacitor rings become a darker shade of it — one texture, any faction tint, no shader needed.
SIZE PROGRESSION
Mk1 is a thin single needle bolt for the agile single-barrel mount. Mk2 pairs two of it for the twin-barrel mount, linked by a short arc. Mk3 is one thick heavy-cored bolt with layered capacitor rings and a star-flare head for the 3-hex heaviest mount.
GODOT SETUP
Export each at 2–4× this scale on transparent PNG. Anchor the origin at the tail (bolt travels toward the head). Set the enemy's projectile Sprite2D.modulate to its faction colour — cyan for Corporate, amber/orange for Pirate, teal for Ancient — same texture throughout.
IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE — GETTING THE MOST OUT OF THESE SPRITES
1. Blend mode & bloom
Draw the sprite on a CanvasItemMaterial with blend_mode = Add (or a light-weight self-illuminating shader). Enable WorldEnvironment → Glow with a threshold around 0.8–1.0 so only the white core and head-flare pixels bloom, not the dimmer grey rib shading. Bloom intensity 0.6–1.0, blend "Screen". This is what turns the flat white core into a hot laser look instead of a printed decal.
2. Motion streak
The sprite is a static bolt, so give it a short stretch on spawn: scale.x from 0.4 → 1 over ~0.05s (an "ease out" pop), then hold. For fast projectiles, add a trailing Line2D or a second half-opacity copy of the same texture offset behind it and fading — cheaper than a particle trail and reads as continuous fire.
3. Muzzle flash & impact
Pair each bolt with a one-shot AnimatedSprite2D at the turret barrel (small radial burst, 3–4 frames, same modulate colour) that plays on fire and fades over ~0.08s. On impact, spawn a brighter, larger copy of the head-flare circle (scale ×2.5, modulate to full white, tween opacity/scale down over 0.15s) plus 4–6 small spark rects flung outward — reuse the existing rib/spark shapes at tiny scale rather than new art.
4. Flicker & pulse
A subtle sine-driven modulate.a wobble (±8%, 20–30Hz) on the core sells "energy" without needing a shader. For the mk3 heavy bolt, pulse scale.y ±5% at a slower 6–8Hz to read as a charged, heavier round.
5. Light2D
Attach a small PointLight2D at the head-flare position with the same tint and a short energy falloff — it casts the tint onto nearby hull plating as the bolt passes, which sells scale far better than the sprite alone, especially in dark corridor scenes.

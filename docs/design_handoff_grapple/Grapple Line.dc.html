THE ROPE
48 verlet nodes, 13px rest length, 12 constraint passes at 120Hz, damping 0.997. Zero gravity, so the only thing shaping it is tension and whatever momentum it was given. The winch node is pinned to the muzzle; everything else is free.
THE WRAP
On contact the hook bites and the next ten nodes are drawn onto a tightening spiral around the hunk over 0.9s — a turn and a half, radius closing to the tile edge. After that the hunk is just another mass on the end of the chain.
SLACK & SNAP
Tension is read from the constraint correction each step. Above 70% the chain brightens and the anchor strains; when it crosses from slack to taut the line cracks straight and both bodies jolt. That readout is the whole feel of the tool.

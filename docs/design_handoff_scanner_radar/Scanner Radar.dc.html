SCOPE — HOW TO READ
BEAM BAR — drag the band to aim, drag the amber arrows to narrow the beam. Narrow beam = higher gain = cleaner trace, further reach.
TRANSMIT LINE — the bright vertical line walking right is the pulse travelling out. 1000 units per second.
PEAKS — a return. Taller = stronger signal (wrecks read loudest, rock quietest). Peaks smear wider the further out they sit, so distant contacts are vague.
NOISE FLOOR — the jitter beneath. It grows with distance; anything not clearing it is not a contact.
Horizontal axis is RANGE, not position. Bearing comes from where you pointed the beam.
